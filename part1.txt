1.

Class: A blueprint for big, complex changes that affect the original object directly.

Struct: A simple box for tiny pieces of data that makes a brand-new copy every time.

Interface: A rulebook or checklist.

Enum: A list of fixed, named options to pick from (like Days of the Week or Game Difficulty: Easy, Medium, Hard).

Main Method: The starting line where a program starts running its very first line of code.
